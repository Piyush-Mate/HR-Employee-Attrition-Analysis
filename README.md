# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition to identify the key factors associated with employees leaving an organization.

The analysis was performed using **Python for data exploration and analysis** and **Power BI for interactive visualization and dashboard creation**.

The goal was to understand attrition patterns across factors such as overtime, job satisfaction, business travel, department, and job role.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the overall employee attrition rate.
* Identify factors associated with higher employee attrition.
* Compare employees who stayed with those who left.
* Analyze attrition across departments and job roles.
* Examine the relationship between overtime and attrition.
* Analyze attrition patterns based on job satisfaction and business travel.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate the findings.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Overtime

Employees who work overtime show a noticeably higher attrition rate compared with employees who do not work overtime.

### Job Role

**Sales Representatives** had the highest attrition percentage among the analyzed job roles.

### Job Satisfaction

Attrition varied across different levels of job satisfaction, showing that employee satisfaction is an important factor to investigate when analyzing turnover.

### Business Travel

Employees with different business travel requirements showed different levels of attrition.

### Department

Attrition patterns also varied across departments, providing useful insight into where employee retention may require additional attention.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights several areas that organizations could investigate when developing employee retention strategies:

* Overtime appears to be strongly associated with employee attrition.
* Certain job roles experience substantially higher attrition than others.
* Employee satisfaction levels are associated with differences in attrition.
* Business travel requirements may influence employee retention.
* Department-level differences can help identify areas requiring further investigation.

These findings can help HR teams focus retention efforts on higher-risk employee groups.
